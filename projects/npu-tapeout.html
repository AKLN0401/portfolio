One-Tile int4 NPU (2x2 MAC Array) for Tiny Tapeout

A 2×2 int4 MAC array with per-row clock gating and a full scan chain, signed off in one 161 × 112 µm SKY130 tile.

Source on GitHub 3D layout

Specs

OVERVIEW
Chip
ProcessSkyWater SKY130 (130 nm), sky130_fd_sc_hd cells
ShuttleTiny Tapeout TTSKY26d, deadline 2026-11-30
Area1×1 tile, 161.0 × 111.52 µm = 17,955 µm²
Top modulett_um_akln0401_npu
Clock50 MHz; setup +5.70 ns, hold +0.162 ns over 12 corners (−40…100 °C, 1.60–1.95 V)
Cells1,595 placed (excl. fill): 98 flops, 2 clock gates, 914 logic gates, 321 timing/fanout buffers, 22 clock-tree cells, 225 taps, 13 diodes
Utilization84.4% · 32.8 mm of routing on met1–met4
Power0.19–0.28 mW at 50 MHz, typical corner, depending on the workload (vector-based estimate, see clock gating)
Accelerator
Array2×2 processing elements, signed int4 × int4
Accumulators4 × 16-bit, two's-complement wrap; 511 worst-case steps without overflow
Per step4 MACs in one clock (outer product acc[i][j] += a[i]·b[j])
ThroughputOne register write ≥ 6 clocks, a step needs 3 writes → ≤ 2.8M steps/s (11M MAC/s) at 50 MHz; the host's GPIO speed sets the real rate
Interface8-bit write data, 8-bit read data, 3-bit address, write strobe; 7 registers
Clock gating1 ICG (dlclkp_1) per row; a row's clock runs only on step/clear cycles. Saves 45–59% of core power (see clock gating)
TestFull scan: 96 flops in one chain, uio[6] → uio[7] (only the 2 reset-sync flops are off it)

Delay-fault detection on a 12-nm RISC-V core

02 / CMOS DESIGN & RELIABILITY · WATERLOO

With Prof. Manoj Sachdev’s CMOS Design and Reliability Group. A flip-flop with adjustable D-Q delay: the same test vectors run at nominal and extended delay, and a transition fault shows up as a different captured value. No shadow register or comparator needed.

GroupCMOS Design and Reliability Group, University of Waterloo
SupervisorProf. Manoj Sachdev
Process node12 nm FinFET
TargetRV32IM RISC-V core
TechniqueVariable D-Q delay flip-flop, transition-fault testing

Only DLY_SEL changes between the two runs.

Razor and the pre-sampling aging sensor add a storage element and comparator at each capture site; the variable-delay flip-flop adds one cell on the launch side.

Razor — Ernst et al., “Razor: Circuit-Level Correction of Timing Errors for Low-Power Operation,” IEEE Micro, Nov–Dec 2004. Pre-sampling aging sensor — Agarwal et al., “Optimized Circuit Failure Prediction for Aging: Practicality and Promise.”

Results

02A / EVALUATION

Implemented on an RV32IM core at 1 GHz in 12 nm FinFET, applied to the most critical paths by slack and compared with established schemes under identical conditions. Every configuration met timing with zero TNS, with lower area and power overhead than the alternatives.

Numbers are held until the paper is published.
